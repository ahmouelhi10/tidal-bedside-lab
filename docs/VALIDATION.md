# Validation status

How the simulator is checked, what is verified, and - above all - what is **not**. Nothing here substitutes for review by a practising anaesthetist or emergency physician.

## 2. UNVERIFIED design parameters (registry status `UNVERIFIED`/`design`)

These are chosen for plausibility or calibrated to a target; none is fitted to patient data. Review each before relying on it clinically.

- Vasopressor PD (norepinephrine, phenylephrine, epinephrine receptor C50s, HR add-ons) and single-compartment kinetics of atropine, nitroglycerin, adenosine, naloxone.
- Drug onset timing (`src/engine/drugs.js`, `Patient._adenosine`; **UNVERIFIED** except where noted): every IV bolus of a single-compartment drug (epinephrine, norepinephrine, phenylephrine, vasopressin, atropine, nitroglycerin, amiodarone, lidocaine, adenosine, calcium chloride, naloxone) reaches the central compartment after a fixed 15 s arm-to-heart transit (`IV_TRANSIT_S`, sourced: Ng 1999 AV block 17.5 +/- 1.0 s after antecubital injection, Watt 1986 ~20 s; not scaled by cardiac output, so it is optimistic during CPR and low-output states). The published PK-PD models (propofol, opioids, relaxants, ketamine, etomidate, midazolam, dexmedetomidine) get no transit: their ke0 was fitted to the response after a bolus and already contains it. Vasopressors, vasodilator, inotrope and antiarrhythmics now act through an effect site with ke0 4/min (t1/2 10 s, design; the arrest model still reads plasma epinephrine); phenylephrine alpha-1 C50 lowered 8 -> 3.5 ng/mL (design) so that 100 mcg still raises MAP 15-40 mmHg once the slower rise lets the baroreflex buffer it. Atropine ke0 0.3 -> 0.65/min so that the effect-site peak falls at 7.7 min (label: maximum heart rate 7-8 min after IV dosing). Adenosine in sinus rhythm (>= 6 mg): high-grade AV block 2.5 s after arrival for 4 s; in SVT the 6 s asystolic pause now also starts after the transit; both response lags and pause lengths are design.
- Propofol haemodynamic coefficients (SVR -42 %, inotropy -25 %, venous tone -30 %, baroreflex blunting at high Ce) - calibrated to typical induction MAP falls.
- Arrest/ROSC model: readiness time constants (90 s up / 240 s down), CPP smoothing (12 s), VF amplitude fade (~8 min), spontaneous-ROSC hazards for PEA/asystole (0.03/0.07 and 0.008/0.02 per min with/without a treated cause), CPR surface loss (20 %), defibrillator charge time and impedance-BMI relation.
- Hypoxic myocardial depression (`myoHypox`) and the hypoxic-arrest trigger; massive-opioid abolition of the hypoxic drive (factor 0.95); naloxone effective at >= 0.2 mg (event-level rule).
- Respiratory: lung volumes (back-fit to Jense 1991 / Farmery-Roe), dead-space and CO2 store sizes, atelectasis shape, age-related shunt, critical DO2, VO2/VCO2 reduction under anaesthesia (-18 %).
- Fluids/thermal: capillary refill kinetics, venous haematocrit defaults (0.40 F / 0.45 M), non-shivering thermogenesis.
- Surgical gestures (all design values, none fitted to patient data): needle margin beyond chest-wall thickness (3 mm), thickness slopes with BMI (1.8/1.7/1.6 mm per unit) and the residual spread (SD 6 + 3.5 mm); tension physiology (airway pressure 14 mmHg at full tension, intrathoracic pressure 1.5x right / 1.0x left, pump depression 45 %, contralateral shunt 0.3, ventilation loss 35 %, CPR flow loss 65 %); the 3x leak enhancement under positive pressure; catheter patency (~7 min mean, shorter with obesity) and the 600 s leak-sealing time constant after a drain; drain rate and malposition (5 % + 10 % x obesity); drain insertion time (2-3 min); front-of-neck success and duration modifiers for obesity, impalpable landmarks and cannula depth; cannula displacement and barotrauma hazards; the cardiac-injury risk flag for long left-lateral catheters (flag only, no physiological consequence is modelled).
- Scoring: weights and time thresholds of every criterion are educational choices, not outcome-validated.
- Realistic patient and hands-on gestures: the 3D body (`assets/patient.glb`), its BMI morph calibration (mesh volume x 1.01 kg/L) and the landmark positions (5th intercostal space 2.5 cm below the nipple line, 2nd intercostal space 5.5 cm below the sternal notch) are visual design estimates on a MakeHuman body, not measured anatomy. They never feed the physiology. The gesture tolerances in `procedures.js` (target radii 2-10 cm, IV cannula 1-3.5 cm, needle 3-8 cm, drain 6-14 cm, incision 2.5-5 cm, 2 s antisepsis hold) are teaching values.
- Tracheal tube and bronchoscopy (`src/engine/ett.js`, all design values): optimal depth 0.1 x height + 4 cm at the incisors with the carina 4 cm beyond; endobronchial (right main bronchus) beyond 0.5 cm past the carina, one-lung shunt 0.28 and alveolar ventilation x 0.92; cuff at the cords above 7 cm (ventilation x 0.85); secretions rising 1/h, obstructive above 0.5 (shunt up to 0.12 per lung, tracheal ventilation loss up to 25 %); scope in the tube ventilation x 0.85; each suction pass de-recruits 0.04 of shunt (time constant 60 s). The airway tree drawn by the bronchoscope (`src/web/gl/airway.js`) uses approximate textbook adult dimensions scaled with height and sex; it is visual only.
- Procedure démarche criteria (`src/engine/demarche.js`, weight 8 per scenario): the step lists follow the cited sequences (Weingart 2012 / DAS 2015 for RSI, AHA 2025 for defibrillation, ATLS for decompression and drains, DAS 2015 for front-of-neck access), but the per-step weights, the 3-min pre-oxygenation and 45-s relaxant ramps, the 10-s CPR-resumption window and the 2-min reassessment window are design values. The `tube_desat` scenario criteria (FiO2 1.0 within 60 s, bronchoscopy within 4 min, tube back in the trachea within 5 min, final tip 2-6 cm above the carina, right-sided suction) and the bronchoscopy démarche (FiO2 before the scope, tube moved only while looking, scope out within 3 min) are design values built on the DOPE mnemonic. Safety steps without a physical trace (check-list, skin antisepsis, "stand clear") are only *declared* by the learner from the guided panel and carry a small share (0.5 of 7-9.5 weight units).

- Infectiology ward course (`src/engine/infection.js`, all design values, none fitted to patient data): bacterial burden half-life 12 h on fully active therapy, logistic regrowth 0.06 /h to a ceiling of 1.5, host response lag 24 h, eradication threshold 0.002 (calibrated so 3 days relapse and 5-7 days do not), an infected central line keeps the burden at 0.4, standard dose of an I agent gives half the killing, oral agents give 60 % while the patient cannot eat; vitals, WBC, CRP (lag 20 h), procalcitonin (lag 10 h), lactate and creatinine are linear maps of the host response; intensive-care transfer after 6 h of severe shock; result timing (Gram stain 1 h, blood culture positive after 9-18 h, identification +18 h, antibiogram +30 h; urine/sputum identification 24 h, antibiogram 48 h; negative blood culture final at 5 days); follow-up blood cultures positive while the burden is above 0.3. Scoring weights and windows (24 h to narrow after the antibiogram, oral switch within 24-96 h of stability, follow-up cultures 36-96 h after the first positive set, the -0.15 penalty for broad Gram-negative agents without MDR risk, the duration bands 6.5-8 / 5-7 / 14-16 days and their decay) are design choices on top of the cited recommendations. The case isolates' MICs are invented but read with the real EUCAST v16.1 breakpoints. Antibiotic activity is all-or-nothing per category (no PK/PD target attainment, no renal dose adjustment, no toxicity, no C. difficile).

- Focused echocardiography (`src/engine/echo.js`, `src/web/echo.js`; **UNVERIFIED**, all design values, none fitted to patient data). Only the cut-offs are sourced (Via 2014, Lang 2015, Rudski 2010, read in the primary PDFs); everything that maps the simulated patient onto an image is a design model: LVEF = sex-specific Lang mean (62 % M, 64 % F) x contractility^1.5 x (1 + 0.003 x (HR - baseline HR)), capped 8-85 %; LV end-diastolic volume = stroke volume / EF and LVEDD = Lang mean (50.2 / 45.0 mm) x (EDV / baseline EDV)^1/3, x up to 1.2 for chronic LV dysfunction; RV basal / mid diameters = Lang means (33 / 27 mm) x (1 + 0.42-0.45 x RV load) x a small filling term; TAPSE = Lang mean 24 mm x (1 - 0.48 x RV load) x a contractility term; septal flattening (D-shaped LV) above RV load 0.35; IVC diameter = 9 + 1.2 x RA pressure mm and sniff collapse = 50 + 8 x (5 - RA pressure) %, quiet-breathing collapse 0.55 of it, ventilated distension 28 - 2.4 x RA pressure % (built so the Rudski category always contains the true RA pressure; tested); pericardial pressure 0.5 x (exp(V / 150 mL) - 1) mmHg (max 30), added to the pressure around the heart like an intrathoracic pressure; effusion depth from a 45 mm-radius spherical shell; RA collapse when pericardial pressure > 2 mmHg and >= 0.45 x RA pressure, RV collapse when > 5 mmHg and >= 0.6 x RA pressure; pericardiocentesis drains 85 %; acute RV pressure overload shifts the RA pressure the LV filling curve sees by 14 mmHg x RV load. Case settings: 27 % blood loss (hypovolaemia), LV function 0.3, 440 mL effusion + 15 mL/kg fluid, RV load 0.7. The probe poses that give each view (marker 315° / 45° / 90° / 90° / 0°, tilt 10 / 10 / 35 / 65 / 45°), the acoustic-window positions on the 3D body (interpolated between the GLB landmarks), the quality tolerances (2-5.5 cm, 45° rotation, 35° tilt, obesity ceiling -25 %), the protocol order (PLAX > PSAX > A4C > subcostal > IVC) and the scoring tolerances (EF +-10 points, TAPSE and IVC +-3 mm, effusion +-4 mm, diagnostic quality >= 70 %) are teaching choices. The image is a schematic Canvas2D drawing (no Doppler: the Nagueh 2016 diastolic cut-offs are listed for reference only).

## 3. Known deviations from literature (deliberate)

- Pre-oxygenation / apnoea test bands were **widened**: SpO2 90 -> 60 % interval 20-70 s; pre-oxygenated (FeO2 ~0.9) vs. 80 % difference ~104 s; propofol HR change +9 %. Reason: published ranges are wide and population-dependent.
- Eleveld V1/Q2 normalisation has two published variants; the engine uses one and documents the choice in `drugs.js`; the other is *unresolved*.
- ATLS haemorrhage bands (AT-BR-1) replaced a primary reference that could not be retrieved; they are used qualitatively only.
- Deliberately **not** used: Absalom's Schnider 77 mg example (unresolved discrepancy), PyTCI `effect_bolus`, Marsh time-to-peak-effect targets.
- Watson male age coefficient fixed to -0.09516 (verified against the original).
- Rocuronium onset ~100 s at 0.74 mg/kg TBW (inside published 60-120 s ranges).
- Drug onset: the brief that prompted this calibration quoted an atropine peak of "2-4 min"; the label (from the Hinderling 1985 PK-PD study) states 7-8 min, which is what the engine now follows. The adenosine sinus-rhythm block drops MAP by ~25 mmHg for a few seconds and the circulation model then overshoots by ~+20 mmHg for ~10 s, whereas the label says 6-12 mg "usually has no systemic hemodynamic effects"; the rebound sinus tachycardia (Watt 1986) is reproduced but the BP overshoot is a model artefact. Not changed for lack of a source: midazolam (label: IV induction in 2-2.5 min, but the model loses consciousness ~55 s after 0.3 mg/kg, and 2.5-5 mg changes RR/MAP by less than the monitor thresholds; the label gives no magnitude), ketamine (sympathomimetic BP rise visible 6 s after the bolus through the Domino ke0), etomidate. The propofol "apnoea at ~89 s" seen in the drug panel is the loss of the capnogram at loss of consciousness (airway obstruction) with the panel's default 2 mg/kg lean-body-mass dose (1.6 mg/kg total weight in a 75 kg man); with the label's 2-2.5 mg/kg it appears at ~45 s. The Diprivan label reports apnoea **durations** (< 30 s in 7 %, 30-60 s in 24 %, > 60 s in 12 % after 2-2.5 mg/kg), not onset times, so no propofol constant was changed.
- State hash quantised to 6 decimals; cross-engine (V8 vs. SpiderMonkey/JSC) equality is expected but not tested.

- Surgical gestures: the tension pneumothorax is unilateral and simple (no haemothorax, no bilateral injury, no mediastinal shift beyond the examination sign); lung sliding and thickness readings are deterministic with a fixed small error; the chest rise asymmetry is reported in the examination text and not drawn on the 3D mesh; the cannula/jet-ventilation path uses a single efficiency of 0.5; attribution of Laan 2016 and the DAS/NAP4 figures rests on secondary sources.

- Infectiology: the WHO AWaRe antibiotic book (2022) was asked for but its full text could not be retrieved (HTTP 403), so no AWaRe category is displayed or scored; "broad" agents are flagged from their spectrum instead. EUCAST states its dosage sheet is not clinical dosing guidance; the simulator shows it because the breakpoints assume those exposures. The vancomycin maintenance dose is shown as the EUCAST regimen (weight-based, drug monitoring) and only the loading dose (25 mg/kg, max 3 g, inside Rybak 2020's 20-35 and obese 20-25 mg/kg) is computed. Cockcroft-Gault is used with actual body weight and is recalled, not re-read (registry UNVERIFIED). Clinical stability uses the ATS/IDSA 2019 criteria as summarised (secondary). BALANCE and FABLED figures come from summaries. Aminoglycoside bracketed breakpoints are shown but the model does not require a companion agent.

- Focused echocardiography: where Lang 2015 and Rudski 2010 differ (RV basal diameter 41 vs 42 mm, TAPSE 17 vs 16 mm) the answer key uses the newer Lang 2015 values and the learner sees both. The RA pressure category is read from Rudski 2010 Table 3 without its secondary indices (Doppler is not simulated). The mapping from FoCUS wording (hyper/normo/hypokinetic, severely hypokinetic) to Lang's EF bands is a design choice. The effusion size is reported in mm without a small/moderate/large grade because no grading was taken from the cited sources. The tamponade case is compensated (MAP about 80-85 mmHg): in this model the circulation fails abruptly once the pericardial pressure approaches the mean systemic filling pressure, so the case stays on the safe side of that cliff.
